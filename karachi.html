CITY

Karachi is the largest city of Pakistan and is often known as the City of Lights. It is the country's main financial and commercial center. Karachi is famous for its beautiful beaches, busy markets, delicious food, and diverse culture. Some popular places include Clifton Beach, Mazar-e-Quaid, and Port Grand. People from different parts of Pakistan live and work in Karachi, making it a city of diversity and opportunities. Karachi also plays an important role in Pakistan's economy because of its seaport, industries, and businesses.
